Projects
------
**Resume Ranking System (RRS)** — my M.Sc. thesis project. A domain-trained word2vec / TF-IDF model of resume vocabulary; cosine similarity matches resumes to job descriptions and k-nearest neighbors clusters candidates. Thesis: [hdl.handle.net/10361/17980](http://hdl.handle.net/10361/17980) · Code: [rrs-app](https://github.com/nishannorunobi/rrs-app), [rrs-fastapi](https://github.com/nishannorunobi/rrs-fastapi), [rrs-core](https://github.com/nishannorunobi/rrs-core)

**Self-hosted workspace platform** — an offline-first Docker platform of 13 containers (PostgreSQL, Plane, Odoo, Redis, RabbitMQ, and an Alloy/Loki/Grafana logging stack) with one-command, health-gated startup and shutdown, verified backups, and an off-site mirror.
