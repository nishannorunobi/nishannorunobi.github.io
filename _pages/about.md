I am a doctoral researcher in Computer Science at the [University of the Potomac](https://potomac.edu/) in Washington, DC. My research sits where **cybersecurity, machine learning, and applied AI** meet: detecting anomalies and insider threats, securing IoT and satellite networks, and understanding how large language models change the way organizations work.

Before research, I spent about a decade building secure software for FinTech and payments — from cryptographic key management and EMV card personalization at Kona Software Lab to identity, event-streaming, and cloud-native banking services at Pubali Bank, and CI/CD and container platforms as a DevOps engineer. That industry work shapes my research: I care about security methods that hold up in production.
